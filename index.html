1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Brine Tycoon is designed as a privacy-first, offline-capable aquarium simulation and tycoon game. We respect your privacy. Brine Tycoon does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or telemetry data.

2. Advertising & Identifiers

Brine Tycoon is 100% ad-free. The application does NOT collect or use the Google Advertising ID (AAID), contains zero third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Data Collection & Permissions

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or log in to play.
  • Local Storage Only: All colony simulation progress, Sea Monkey genetics, aquarium decorations, and coins are stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database (Room).
  • Notifications (Optional): On Android 13+, the app may request the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission solely to notify you when aquarium water chemistry requires attention (e.g. low oxygen or extreme salinity). These alerts are scheduled locally on your device without communicating with any external server.
  • In-App Purchases (Optional): Transactions are handled securely directly by the Google Play Store through Google Play Billing (BillingClient 8.0.0). We never see or store your financial or payment details.

4. Children’s Privacy

Brine Tycoon complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. Because the app collects zero personal data, it poses no privacy risks to users of any age.
