# Privacy Policy for Brine Tycoon

## 1. Overview
Solid Take Games built **Brine Tycoon** as an offline, ad-free aquarium simulation game. This service is provided at no cost to play, with optional one-time purchases via Google Play Billing.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**We do not collect any personal data.**  
Brine Tycoon does not require account creation, sign-in, or social network integration. All simulation data, colony records, and tank parameters remain strictly on your local device.

## 3. In-App Purchases
Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing** (v8.0.0). We do not collect, process, or store payment details.

## 4. Permissions
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (Android 13+): Optional local device notifications for water chemistry drift alerts. Handled entirely on-device without remote servers.

## 5. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising
Brine Tycoon contains **no ads** and **no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs**.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Brine Tycoon complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policies. No personal information is collected from any users, including children.
